# SaMeya Collection - Online Fashion Store

## Overview
An elegant online fashion store for SaMeya Collection featuring a luxurious gold-on-black design aesthetic. The website showcases fashion products with sophisticated branding and provides a complete shopping experience without payment processing.

## Core Features

### Product Catalog
- Browse products organized by categories: Dresses, Accessories, New Arrivals
- Display product grid with images, names, and prices
- Filter and search functionality within categories
- Featured products section on homepage

### Product Details
- Individual product pages with high-quality images
- Detailed product descriptions, sizes, colors, and pricing
- Image gallery with multiple product views
- Related products suggestions

### Shopping Cart
- Add products to cart with quantity selection
- View cart contents with item details and totals
- Update quantities and remove items
- Cart persistence across browser sessions
- Checkout preparation (no payment processing)

### Brand Information
- About SaMeya Collection page with brand story and mission
- Company values and design philosophy
- Brand heritage and craftsmanship details

### Navigation & Layout
- Top navigation bar with logo, menu, and cart icon
- Responsive design optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Footer with contact information, social links, and policies
- Luxurious gold and black color scheme throughout

## Backend Data Storage
- Product catalog with details, images, categories, and inventory
- Product categories and subcategories
- Brand content for About page
- Basic site configuration and settings

## Backend Operations
- Retrieve product listings by category
- Get individual product details
- Search products by name or description
- Manage product inventory and availability
- Serve brand content and company information
